## Brand & Style

This design system targets East African primary-to-secondary school transition programs (Pre-Form One / Maandalizi ya Kidato cha Kwanza). It serves three distinct user cohorts: young adolescent learners navigating unfamiliar secondary school concepts (English immersion, introductory sciences, foundational mathematics), classroom instructors tracking diagnostic competencies, and parents monitoring student milestones across multilingual interfaces.

The visual direction follows a **Modern Academic & Warm Humanist** ethos. It replaces punitive, institutional bureaucratics with encouraging, clarity-first digital spaces. The emotional baseline balances institutional authority (fostering parental and ministerial trust) with youthful optimism and psychological safety (reducing anxiety for students stepping into English-medium secondary instruction).

Key architectural tenets:
- **Calm Authority & Vitality**: Deep maritime blues establish structured rigor, while verdant growth tones celebrate micro-achievements, daily streaks, and skill masteries.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium**: Layouts natively support variable string expansions between Swahili and English without layout rupture or clumsy ellipsis truncation.
- **Contextual Tactility**: Surface treatments favor clean, airy geometries enhanced with subtle, micro-textured geometric motifs derived from traditional East African kitenge tessellations and geometric graph paper grids.

## Colors

The system uses a deliberate four-tier chromatic balance engineered for daytime classroom visibility, outdoor mobile connectivity, and accessible readouts on varied screen hardware.

### Primary (`#0F2942`)
The structural anchor. Represents institutional permanence, scholarship, and focus. Used for primary top-level navigation, active tab boundaries, prominent hero headers, and high-emphasis interface controls. Deep shades avoid stark pure-black eye strain during intensive study sessions.

### Secondary (`#0D9488` with `#10B981` highlight support)
The catalyst color denoting active progress, growth, correct answers, syllabus completion percentages, and positive instructional reinforcement (*Maendeleo*). Provides an energetic counterbalance to deep navy without introducing chaotic neon saturation.

### Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)
An amber-gold accent inspired by morning savannah sunlight and academic distinction ribbons. Reserved strictly for achievement tokens, badges (*Tuzo / Nishani*), critical reminders, assignment deadlines, and starred revision items.

### Neutrals & Backgrounds
- Base Canvas: Pristine off-white (`#F8FAFC`) to minimize glare under high ambient light conditions.
- Card & Panel Surfaces: Pure clean white (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine neutral cool grey boundaries (`#E2E8F0`).
- Text Primary: Deep obsidian slate (`#0F172A`) assuring WCAG AAA contrast ratios.
- Text Secondary: Subdued graphite (`#475569`) for metadata, bilingual subtitles, and secondary timestamps.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the exclusive typographic workhorse across all levels. Its tall x-height, broad apertures, and geometric structure ensure instant legibility for young students transitioning from Kiswahili-medium primary education into fast-paced English secondary instruction. 

Typography specifications:
- **Headlines & Display**: Set with medium-to-tight letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with strong semi-bold and extra-bold weights to anchor section goals (*Mada za Wiki / Weekly Topics*).
- **Body & Explanatory Text**: Generous vertical line-height (`1.5x` to `1.6x`) prevents visual line collision during bilingual term comparisons (e.g., *Mlinganyo wa Mistari / Linear Equations*).
- **Localized Label Treatment**: Micro-labels, badge texts, and status pills always use uppercase or distinct semi-bold casing to maintain distinction against standard paragraph phrasing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured **12-column fluid-responsive grid** pinned to a maximum container boundary of `1240px`, ensuring dashboards do not over-stretch across ultra-wide monitors in school administrative centers.

Responsive breakpoints and behavior:
- **Mobile (320px – 639px)**: 4-column system with `16px` margins and `16px` gutters. Lesson navigation shifts into a persistent bottom navigation bar or stacked full-width cards. All buttons span full container width for simplified thumb actuation.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px)**: 8-column system with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters. Two-column split-view architectures emerge (e.g., lesson directory on the left, interactive quiz pane on the right).
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column layout with `32px` to `48px` margins and `24px` gutters. Dedicated collapsible vertical rail for teacher/student navigation, an expansive content core, and contextual student metric summary panels.

Spacing adheres strictly to an 8-point base rhythm, with a 4-point micro-scale reserved for fine label alignments, iconography cushions, and tag padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, artificial drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Atmospheric Tinted Shadows**. Surfaces capture depth by integrating subtle cool slate and navy color tints into soft, extended shadow radii, mimicking the natural ambient diffusion of classroom daylight.

Elevation ladder:
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC`. May feature an ultra-faint watermark overlay (1.5% to 2% opacity) depicting subtle geometric mathematical grids or stylized education iconography (open books, compasses, foliage).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface lifted with an extra-crisp boundary outline (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards, Hover States, Floating Controls)**: Elevated card states with a deeper presence: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Drawers, Notifications)**: Heavy focus elevation: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.10), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`, paired with a tinted backdrop scrim (`#0F2942` at 45% opacity with an optional `4px` backdrop blur).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an approachable, friendly **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to evoke friendliness, safety, and modern software ergonomics for young students.

Corner radius mapping:
- Micro elements (Tags, badges, checkboxes, small inputs): `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Medium interactive elements (Primary buttons, text field containers, tabs): `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px).
- Macro containers (Assignment cards, lesson modules, assessment summary panels): `1rem` (16px).
- Modals, prominent popups, and floating sheets: `1.25rem` (20px) to `1.5rem` (24px).
- Avatars, status indicators, and pill tags: `9999px` (Full circular pill).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Deep Navy `#0F2942` fill, text in `#FFFFFF`, `12px` vertical by `24px` horizontal padding, `10px` rounded corners. Hover state activates a subtle shift to `#1E3A8A` with a delicate elevated shadow.
- **Secondary / Action Accent Button**: Teal `#0D9488` background, white text, designed for positive forward momentum (e.g., *Anza Somo / Start Lesson*, *Wasilisha / Submit*). Hover transitions to `#0F766E`.
- **Tertiary / Outline Button**: Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F2942`. Active or hover states introduce a light tint fill (`rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`).

### Chips & Badges
- Status badges use a soft background fill paired with high-contrast text.
- **Progress / Pass**: Background `#CCFBF1`, text `#0F766E` (*Imekamilika / Completed*).
- **Pending / Revision**: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309` (*Inasubiri / In Review*).
- **Subject Identifier Chips**: Compact tags with light navy fills (`#E2E8F0`), text in `#0F2942`, used for quick indexing (*Basic Mathematics*, *English Grammar*, *Biology Foundations*).

### Input Fields
- Container styled with `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`, smooth `#FFFFFF` background, and `10px` border radius.
- Default text in `#0F172A`, placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state eliminates standard blue browser rings, applying a `2px` border in `#0D9488` coupled with an ambient glow ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`.
- Helper labels and validation states support inline dual-language hints.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` fill, `16px` border radius, and a crisp `1px` boundary (`#E2E8F0`).
- Lesson progress cards feature an integrated top accent line or subtle left indicator border in `#0D9488` or `#F59E0B` to signal active student standing.
- Padding defaults to `24px` on desktop and scales cleanly to `16px` on mobile viewports.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- `20px x 20px` touch targets with `6px` corner rounding for checkboxes and pure circles for radios.
- Unselected: Crisp `2px` boundary `#CBD5E1` on white background.
- Selected: Solid `#0D9488` fill with a sharp, bold white checkmark icon or center dot.

### Modal Dialogs
- Centered overlay container framed with `20px` corner radius, pure white surface, and Level 3 ambient elevation.
- Backdrop scrim consists of `#0F2942` at `50%` opacity with a subtle `4px` blur effect to eliminate background visual noise.
- Modals feature a distinct dual-tone header zone: title rendered in `headline-sm` with an adjoining Swahili sub-label, dedicated content body, and clear stacked or horizontally aligned action triggers pinned to the base.

### Specialized Module: Bilingual Language Switcher
- A pill-shaped segmented toggle positioned at the top navigation boundary.
- Active language pill highlights in `#0F2942` with `#FFFFFF` text, inactive language rests on subtle `#F1F5F9` with `#475569` text, facilitating instantaneous toggling between Kiswahili and English curriculum modes.
